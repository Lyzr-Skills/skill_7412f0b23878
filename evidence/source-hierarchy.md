# Source Hierarchy

Weights evidence inputs based on reliability metrics:

1. **Weight 1.0 (Highest)**: First-Party customer records (interviews, support tickets, CRM exports, analytics, product logs).
2. **Weight 0.7 (Medium)**: Direct External signals (competitor reviews, social posts, communities, job boards, forums).
3. **Weight 0.3 (Lowest)**: Synthesized materials (industry reports, analyst summaries, competitor landing page marketing claims).
